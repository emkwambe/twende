You are the **exact profile** this award was created for:

- ✅ **US-based business** (Mpingo Systems LLC, Charlotte, NC)

- ✅ **Trade/investment relationship** (fintech platform connecting US infrastructure to African markets)

| Attribute | Details |

| **Type** | Non-dilutive grant |
| **Deadline** | **Rolling applications** [^186^] |
| **Focus** | Grassroots organizations and small enterprises in underserved African communities |
| **Process** | Application → Due diligence → Grant agreement → Milestone-based disbursement |

### How to Position TWENDE

Frame TWENDE as a **diaspora-led bridge** between US financial technology expertise and African informal economy participants:
