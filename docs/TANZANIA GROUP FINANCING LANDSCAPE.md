## 1\. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Tanzania has $122 million+ in active credit guarantee capital and multiple government/international programs specifically designed for organized groups. The single biggest barrier is not lack of funds — it's documentation. 54.3% of SMEs have no proper accounting records. VICOBA groups struggle with constitution writing, minute-keeping, and financial reporting. Twende solves exactly this.

## 2\. GOVERNMENT OF TANZANIA PROGRAMS

### 2.1 Tanzania Agricultural Development Bank (TADB)

Table

|  |  |
| :---- | :---- |
| What | State-owned development bank for agriculture |
| Key Product | Smallholder Credit Guarantee Scheme (SCGS) — $25M fund |
| Mechanism | 50% credit risk guarantee \+ 50% liquidity to partner banks |
| Interest Rate | 8–12% for smallholders (vs. market 19–30%) |
| Who Qualifies | Smallholder farmers, agri-SMEs, farmer groups/cooperatives |
| Documents Required | Group registration, bank account, farm records, IDs |
| Impact | 11,200+ direct beneficiaries, 755,000+ indirect |

*"Grouping of farmers as cooperatives, associations or out-grower groups helps to reduce transaction costs for PFIs."* — FSDT Guarantee Scheme Review

Twende Angle: TADB's SCGS explicitly covers group lending. A Twende-registered                                     VICOBA with financial records is directly eligible.

## 7\. FIRST TARGET: TADB SCGS \+ SIDO

Why these two:

1. TADB SCGS is active, well-funded ($25M+), and explicitly designed for farmer groups  
2. SIDO helps groups prepare the exact financial reports banks require  
3. Both are government-backed — credibility for users  
4. Both require group documentation — Twende's core value prop

*"Register your VICOBA on Twende. We'll help you write your constitution, track every share and loan, and auto-generate the quarterly reports TADB requires. When you apply for the Smallholder Credit Guarantee Scheme, your paperwork is already done."*
